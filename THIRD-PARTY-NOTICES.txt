Third-Party Notices — Admin Toolkit for Salesforce
====================================================

(force-sheet-univer.bundle.js and force-sheet-univer.bundle.css) built with
Vite from the Univer sheets-core preset (https://univer.ai) and the
open-source packages listed below. Each package is the work of its authors
and is licensed under the license shown next to it. The full text of each
license follows the package list. This file is provided to satisfy the
